# 📖 Overview

Saarthi AI is an enterprise-grade Sales Intelligence Platform that automates company research before every customer meeting.

Instead of spending valuable time searching company websites, financial reports, news articles, LinkedIn pages, and market trends manually, Saarthi AI performs the entire research process automatically and generates a comprehensive AI-powered briefing.

Sales professionals receive a complete meeting summary containing company background, latest developments, business opportunities, potential risks, suggested conversation points, executive insights, and market intelligence—all in a single dashboard.

The platform significantly reduces manual preparation time while improving sales readiness, customer engagement, and decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Modern sales representatives spend considerable time gathering information from multiple sources before every customer interaction.

The process typically involves:

- Visiting company websites
- Reading news articles
- Checking LinkedIn profiles
- Understanding recent funding
- Reviewing products
- Searching competitors
- Collecting financial information

This repetitive manual workflow reduces productivity and often leads to incomplete or outdated information.

Saarthi AI eliminates this challenge by automatically collecting, processing, analyzing, and summarizing company intelligence before every meeting.

---

# 💡 Solution

Saarthi AI combines workflow automation with Generative AI to deliver contextual meeting intelligence.

The platform continuously gathers information from multiple business sources, processes it using AI, and presents sales representatives with concise, actionable insights.

Instead of spending 30–60 minutes researching, users receive a complete AI-generated briefing within seconds.

Saarthi AI automatically generates comprehensive meeting briefings by combining information from multiple trusted sources into a single, easy-to-read summary.

Before every scheduled meeting, the platform analyzes the target company and prepares contextual insights that help sales representatives understand the business before speaking with the customer.

- Products & Services
- Industry Analysis

# 🔄 Workflow

1. User logs into Saarthi AI.

2. Google Calendar retrieves upcoming meetings.

3. Company name is extracted automatically.

4. n8n workflow begins collecting data.

5. Company information is gathered.

6. Latest news is collected.

7. Business intelligence is analyzed.

8. Gemini AI summarizes all information.

9. Flask processes the response.

10. Dashboard displays the final AI briefing.
